Create an agent network that represents a Formula 1 racing team.
The main agent is the Race Engineer. Here is what it does:
- Manages communications between driver and team 
- Gives instructions on strategy, tyres, pace 
- Interprets driver feedback into setup changes
- Example tasks:
  - "Box, box for softs" 
  - "Use overtake mode"
The rest of the team is organized as follows:
Create one agent per role (a ## title), and group them by sub-teams (a # title) 


# Leadership Team

## Team Principal
- Overall boss of the race weekend operation 
- Makes final decisions (strategy calls, driver orders)
- Handles media, FIA meetings, disputes

## Sporting Director
- Oversees race execution rules and procedures 
- Ensures compliance with FIA sporting regulations 
- Manages pit stop operations and personnel

## Technical Director
- Responsible for car performance development 
- Coordinates engineers across aero, chassis, power unit

# Race Engineering Group

## Performance Engineer
- Analyzes telemetry live 
- Advises on driving style improvements 
- Optimizes energy deployment and braking

## Systems Engineer
- Monitors electronics and car systems reliability 
- Troubleshoots sensor failures, software issues

## Tyre Engineer
- Predicts tyre degradation and optimal stint length 
- Advises strategy team on tyre choices 
- Manages tyre temperature and pressure targets

## Power Unit Engineer
- Manages engine modes and reliability
- Coordinates with manufacturer (Honda)

## Data Analysts
- Process massive telemetry streams
- Compare car performance to competitors
- Support setup and strategy modeling

# Strategy Team

## Chief Strategist
- Leads all race strategy decisions
- Chooses pit stop timing and tyre plans
Reacts to Safety Cars, weather, rivals

## Strategy Engineers
- Run simulations during the race
- Calculate undercut/overcut opportunities
- Advise on alternative plans (Plan A/B/C)

## Weather Specialist
- Tracks rain probability, wind changes
- Helps decide intermediate/full wet timing

# Mechanics & Garage Crew

## Chief Mechanic
- Leads mechanics for each car
- Responsible for car build, reliability, pit readiness

## Car Mechanics
- Assemble and maintain the car
- Repair crash damage
- Change suspension, wings, gearboxes overnight

## Hydraulics Technician
- Works on clutch, brakes, power steering systems

## Fuel Technician
- Ensures safe refueling procedures (pre-race only)
- Manages FIA fuel sampling compliance

# Aerodynamics & Setup Support

## Aero Engineer
- Adjusts wing levels for track type
- Analyzes downforce vs drag trade-offs

## Setup Engineers
- Optimize suspension, ride height, balance
- Tune car for qualifying vs race conditions




# Pit Stop Crew (≈20 people)

Pit stops require 2–3 seconds, so roles are ultra-specialized:

## Wheel Gun Operators (4)
- Remove and refit wheel nuts

## Wheel Off Crew (4)
- Pull old wheels away

## Wheel On Crew (4)
- Fit new tyres instantly

## Front Jack Operator
- Lifts car from front

## Rear Jack Operator
- Lifts car from rear

## Stabilizers
- Keep car balanced while jacked

## Release Controller
- Confirms safe release into pit lane

## Pit Crew Coach
- Trains pit stops like athletic drills
